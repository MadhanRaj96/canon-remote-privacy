Privacy Policy for Canon Remote

Effective Date: July 23, 2026

Madhan Raj Jayabalan ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Canon Remote mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information Collection and Use

Our App is designed to function locally as a remote control and intervalometer for compatible Canon cameras using Bluetooth.

Device Permissions Required

To establish and maintain a connection with your camera, the App requests access to specific device features:

  • Bluetooth / Nearby Devices (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN): Required strictly to discover, pair with, and transmit shutter/timer signals to your Canon camera.
  • Location Access (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): On certain operating system versions (such as older Android versions), system requirements mandate Location permission to perform Bluetooth scanning. We do not track, collect, store, or transmit your actual location data. This permission is used solely to enable the system-level Bluetooth scan for nearby cameras.

Personal Data

We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, camera photos, or device identifiers).

2. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising frameworks that collect, monitor, or analyze user data.

3. Data Storage and Transmission

All intervalometer settings, shutter timers, and connection configurations remain strictly on your local mobile device. No data is sent to external servers or cloud services.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Effective Date."
